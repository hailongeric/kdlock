# Every workload and worker count

Rates are Mops/s. Whole-run tables use three-round medians; peak tables use the highest valid warmed window of at least 300 ms. B/S is KD blocking divided by KD spin. CPU cost is summed worker CPU nanoseconds per completed operation. It is not acquisition latency.

[Statistics](statistics.csv) retain min/median/max/spread for every arm and metric; [samples](samples.csv) retain all individual rounds. Shaded plot ranges are the three observed rounds, not confidence intervals.

JIT counts give the number of rounds with returned JIT bodies. A zero is a native-path control; a NORMAL publication alone is insufficient. Spin/rwlock policy pairs are regression controls: the mutex parameter does not change those algorithms.
